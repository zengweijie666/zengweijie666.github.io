BrandOS Privacy Policy

This policy describes how BrandOS will handle Google Ads data when the Keyword Planner integration is enabled. BrandOS is an internal ecommerce operations workspace.

Data we access

BrandOS will use Google OAuth to connect an authorized Google Ads account. To run a keyword query, BrandOS will send Google the target account ID, seed terms or a public product page URL, and search settings such as country, language, and network. Google Ads may return keyword suggestions, search volume, and related historical metrics.

How we use and store data

BrandOS will display keyword results to authorized operators for product feed research. Operators may retain selected keyword evidence with a product draft for review. BrandOS will use the account identifiers and OAuth credentials to make authorized requests and keep credentials on the application server.

Who can access the data

Access to the keyword results and saved evidence will follow BrandOS store permissions. BrandOS will send query inputs to Google Ads to obtain results. The Keyword Planner integration will use Google Ads data for the feature described here and will not sell it or make it publicly available. BrandOS will handle information received from Google APIs in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Revocation and data requests

You can revoke BrandOS access in your Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future requests using that connection. Saved keyword evidence can remain with its product draft while needed for review. To request access to or deletion of saved Google Ads data, contact the team through the Palay contact page and mention BrandOS.
