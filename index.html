BrandOS

BrandOS is an internal workspace for teams that manage online stores. It brings Shopify product details and Google Merchant Center feed records together so operators can compare listings, prepare edits, and review drafts before publication.

Google Ads keyword research

BrandOS is preparing a Keyword Planner integration for the Palay operations team. With access to an authorized Google Ads account, operators will be able to look up keyword ideas and historical search metrics. They can use those results to evaluate wording for relevant product listings during feed review.

The pilot will query keyword data for research. Operators review proposed product wording in BrandOS before any separate publishing step.

Read the BrandOS Privacy Policy for details on how the integration handles Google Ads data.
